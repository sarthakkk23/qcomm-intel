07What this is, and isn't

  • Attention, not salesEvery number is a public signal of attention: searches, pageviews, news and app ratings. None of it measures orders, revenue or market share.
  • SearchGoogle Trends' relative index, shown as each brand's share of the four. Instamart is searched as "Instamart", which also counts "Swiggy Instamart".
  • WikipediaBlinkit and Zepto only: Instamart and Flipkart Minutes have no article of their own.
  • Play StoreStandalone apps. Flipkart Minutes' app launched in September 2026 as a limited beta, so most of its usage still happens inside the Flipkart app.
  • NewsGDELT throttles requests and skips some days. Missing days stay blank, never zero, and news share appears only when all four brands report.
  • Refresh and rulesCollected daily by GitHub Actions, usually by midday IST. Insights compare full weeks and stay silent when there isn't enough data.
